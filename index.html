About Akasha’s Studio

Your vision, brought to life.

Welcome to Akasha’s Studio, a creative space where ideas come to life, little details matter, and every design begins with a vision.

What started as a personal passion for designing gradually grew into something I wanted to share with others. After spending years creating designs for clients, I took a step towards turning that passion into a space of its own, one where creativity could be explored, ideas could take shape, and people could find designs that felt just right for them.

And that’s how Akasha’s Studio became what it is today: a creative space built around thoughtful design, attention to detail, and the joy of bringing ideas to life.

The Heart Behind the Studio

I’m Akasha, the founder and designer behind Akasha’s Studio.

I’ve always appreciated the way thoughtful design can transform an idea into something beautiful. The right colours, carefully chosen typography, and the smallest details can come together to create something truly special. This is the creativity I strive to bring to every project I take on.

From personalized wedding invitations that become part of cherished celebrations to creative visuals that help businesses express their identity, I enjoy creating designs that are not only visually appealing but also meaningful to the people they are made for.

For me, one of the most rewarding parts of the process is seeing an idea take shape: understanding a client’s vision, exploring creative possibilities, and bringing everything together in a design they can proudly call their own.

Our Approach to Design

At Akasha’s Studio, I believe that every design should have a purpose and every project deserves a thoughtful approach.

Every client has a unique vision, set of preferences, and specific requirements. My aim is to understand these details and translate them into designs that reflect the intended style, purpose, and personality of each project.

My approach is guided by three key principles:

Creativity with Purpose

Creating designs that communicate the right message while maintaining a cohesive and visually appealing aesthetic.

Attention to Detail

Carefully considering colours, typography, composition, and the finer elements that bring each design together.

A Personalized Experience

Working collaboratively with clients to understand their ideas and create designs tailored to their preferences and needs.

What We Create

Akasha’s Studio offers creative design services for life’s special moments and growing businesses.

From personalized digital wedding invitations for Nikkah, Mehndi, Barat, Walima, Engagements, and other celebrations to business graphics and social media designs, each project is an opportunity to explore new ideas and create something thoughtfully tailored to its purpose.

Whether you’re preparing for a special occasion or looking to communicate your brand through creative visuals, the goal is to bring your ideas to life through designs that feel considered, distinctive, and personal.

Creating Something Meaningful, Together

Akasha’s Studio continues to grow through creativity, new ideas, and the trust of the clients who choose to be part of this journey. It is a space where I can continue to explore my passion for design, develop new creative possibilities, and help others bring their visions to life.

I’m grateful to everyone who has supported my work and trusted me with their ideas. Every project brings something new, and every opportunity to create for someone is a reminder of why I started in the first place.

Whether you have a clear vision or are just beginning to explore ideas, I’d love to hear what you have in mind and work with you to create something special.

Your vision, thoughtfully designed.

How to Order

  1. Get in touch. Message me on Instagram or Facebook, or send an email.
  2. Share your details. Event details, preferred theme, colour palette, number of pages and any inspiration images.
  3. Receive a proposal. I’ll send a design proposal and quotation.
  4. Confirm your order. Approve the quote and send the required details.
  5. Review and revise. Check the design and request revisions within the agreed scope.
  6. Receive your files. Your final digital files are delivered to you.

The exact process, payment arrangements, delivery time and revision policy are confirmed with you before an order is accepted.
